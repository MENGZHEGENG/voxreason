# Figure 2 brief — evidence sensitivity, revision v2

Takeaway: the declared counterfactual and source-label oracle satisfy the
required-change and preservation checks, while controls that lack the decisive
cue do not.

Question: How do the six audited conditions behave on the source-key-disjoint
holdout when the utterance is fixed and one cue changes?

Figure class: four-panel empirical bar plot.

Evidence path: `runs/evidence_sensitivity/summary.json`, generated by
`scripts/run_evidence_sensitivity.py`. The plot uses seed `0`, which is
identical to the other deterministic seeds in this fixture. Bars are means over
72 valid records per condition. Whiskers are the released 95% source-group
bootstrap intervals (`bootstrap.samples = 10000`, seed `20260911`). No smoothing,
normalization, or imputation is applied; incomplete learned-run outputs are
excluded by the audited summary.

Metric semantics: plan-slot accuracy, required-change accuracy, and preservation
rate are higher-is-better; unexpected-change rate is lower-is-better. Values are
unitless rates in [0, 1]. Exact source condition descriptions remain in the
summary and manuscript caption.

Contract: fixed 180 mm x 104 mm canvas, vector PDF/SVG plus 300 dpi PNG preview,
constrained layout, serif typography aligned with the manuscript, and no tight
bounding-box crop. The final PDF is checked at intended placement and enlarged
for clipping, label overlap, interval visibility, and grayscale legibility.
